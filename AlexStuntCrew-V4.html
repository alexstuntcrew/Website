01 The community

About AlexStuntCrew

AlexStuntCrew is built around stunt riding, progression, community and making riding culture more respectful and welcoming.

A rider. A crew. A purpose.

More than wheelies

The goal is not only to improve as a rider. It is also to create content, test products honestly and use the platform to support positive projects.

Every crash, repair, cleanup and riding session becomes part of the story.

Safety disclaimer

Good riding. Clear information.

Ride responsibly

Stunt riding is dangerous and may result in serious injury, property damage or death. Content shown on this website is not an instruction to copy any stunt. Always wear suitable protective equipment, follow local laws and ride only in safe and permitted areas. Product results may vary depending on the bike, installation, impact and riding conditions.

Fit, installation & product limitations ＋

Confirm compatibility before ordering. The listed product has an adhesive-mounting base; a universal-fit description is not a guarantee that it fits every handlebar. Obtain suitable installation guidance, check that the throttle, grips and controls move freely, and inspect attachment before each ride and after any impact. Stop using a loose or damaged part. Bar ends cannot prevent every injury or every instance of bike damage.

Orders & consumer rights ＋

Orders are arranged through the listed contact channels; this website has no checkout. Before agreeing to a purchase, request the seller’s identity, product and manufacturer details, compatibility, total price including any delivery charges, payment and delivery arrangements, and applicable cancellation, return and legal-guarantee information. Nothing on this website excludes mandatory consumer rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Community, locations & media ＋

A location appearing in a photo is not permission to ride there or an invitation to an organised event. Obtain the landowner’s permission and follow local rules. Respect neighbours and other riders. Do not handle needles, chemicals or other hazardous waste during cleanups; contact the relevant local service. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners. Sponsorships, collaborations and gifts are identified separately. For an image, credit or privacy concern, contact EbikeStr by email.

Privacy & saved preferences ＋

This page does not run analytics, advertising trackers or embedded social feeds. Your chosen language, theme and saved-product preference are stored locally in your browser. Use “Reset saved preferences” to clear them. GitHub Pages hosting may process connection information such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement. External websites and email providers apply their own privacy policies. Contact EbikeStr at ebikest@mail.ru about privacy or content concerns.
